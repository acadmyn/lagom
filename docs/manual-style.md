# lagom manual: style

The operator's manual (LGM-OM-01) is an HTML artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/UYohsM5dnj5sjDbStq3i8g

## Writing

- No filler. Cut every word that doesn't change the meaning.
- Every sentence carries information. One fact per sentence.
- Get to the point: action or fact first.
- Short words, fewer words.
- One example at most, usually none.
- No stock phrases ("it's important to note", "simply", "allows you to", "in order to").
- No transitions unless order is the information.
- Imperative for actions ("hold SOLO, press BKR"), present tense for facts ("fader sets density").
- Lowercase everywhere (after teenage engineering's EP-133 guide). Codes and control names stay in caps.
- Controls are shown as key caps. `+` = hold the first, press the second. A bar above a cap = hold. `‡` = lands on the next bar.
- Tables over prose. A chapter opens with at most two sentences.
- No hedging, no marketing tone, no exclamation marks.

## Design

- Apollo checklist structure, printed like a 70s Braun/Roland manual. No sci-fi styling, no ageing effects.
- A4 sheets on a desk, laid out exactly as printed. The panel sheet is landscape. Sizes inside a sheet are in mm.
- Cool grey-white paper. Only the desk changes in dark mode.
- Three inks, each meaning one layer: black = normal, orange = SOLO held, blue = BANK LEFT held. Colour means nothing else.
- Type: Archivo across its width axis (condensed legends, normal body, wide heads). IBM Plex Mono for codes and numbers only.
- Drawing: front view traced from the hardware, hairline outlines, flat grey only on moving parts. Hardware's own printing in grey caps.
- Control codes: `F1–F8`, `FM`, `K1.1–K8.3` (track.row), `M1–M8`, `R1–R8`, `SOLO`, `BKL`, `BKR`, `SEND`.
- Running header, footer with page `1-2`, document number and issue letter.
- Chapters: 1 control surface, 2 screen and keys, 3 concepts, 4 procedures, 5 patch sheet, 6 troubleshooting.
